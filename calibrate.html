Calibrate simulation

This page learns how fast your oven heats and cools from a recorded log. The best calibration cycle is one uninterrupted full-power warmup followed by cooling with the heater off, keeping the door and fans unchanged.

Select one warmup-and-cooldown section, or just a heater-off cooldown. For a long cycle, choose a suggestion and zoom in to adjust it. Skip programmed ramps and holds. Room temperature is assumed to be 25°C.
